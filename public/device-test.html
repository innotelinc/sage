SAGE capture test

This page asks this device one question: which of Listen’s two sources can it actually open? Press a button and answer the sheet. Each test reads about four seconds of sound, measures it, encodes a two-second clip with the same recorder the app uses, then stops everything and keeps nothing. No audio is uploaded.

microphone permission: checking…
Source Result What happened
  1. Lecture needs a microphone. A green result with peak=0 is the shell agreeing and the room being silent, which is not a failure.
  2. Audiobook needs getDisplayMedia with an audio track. Browsers grant it from a share sheet: pick the tab or screen that is playing, and tick “share audio”. A shell that returns video with no audio says so rather than passing silently.
  3. Refused means the container said no — in the iOS app that is the WebView, not the phone, so open this same page in Chrome on that phone to tell the two apart.
  4. Rejected the options means the method exists and the arguments do not: the page retries once with { video, audio } alone and reports both answers.
  5. The report at the bottom is plain text, so it can be pasted into an issue or a message without the screenshot.
