What a session is

A session is a fifteen-minute window of access to the model gateway, bought in advance. One session is one key: it works for as many requests as you like until the window closes, and the window starts when the purchase is confirmed. Sessions are not subscriptions and do not renew by themselves.

Buying

  • On the web and Android the purchase is taken by our payment provider. Refund requests made within fourteen days go to dhunter@innotel.us, and an unused session is refunded in full.
  • Prices are shown before you pay, in the currency your store or payment provider reports.
