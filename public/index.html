Watching the screen

An app cannot read its own screen, and recording fights video playback for the microphone — that is why listening stops the moment a lesson video plays. Apple's own screen recorder does not have that problem.

  1. Swipe down from the top-right corner and tap the record button. Add it once in Settings → Control Center.
  2. Play the lesson. The recording keeps running while the video plays.
  3. Stop the recording, come back to Sage, then tap Import the recording below.
