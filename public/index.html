Settings

Gateway

OmniRoute keys only. The key is checked against gateway.innotel.us/v1/models before it is stored, and rejected keys are never saved. It stays on this device.

Session

A session is a timed key: pay once, then use the gateway as much as you like until the window closes.

On iPhone the purchase goes through the App Store: Apple charges the Apple ID on this phone, and the key installs here the moment Apple confirms it.

Payment is Stripe's, run by Magnate; the key is minted the moment it confirms and installs itself here. Lost the receipt? Claim the code from it below.

Access

Watching the screen

An app cannot read its own screen, and recording fights video playback for the microphone — that is why listening stops the moment a lesson video plays. Apple's own screen recorder does not have that problem.

  1. Swipe down from the top-right corner and tap the record button. Add it once in Settings → Control Center.
  2. Play the lesson. The recording keeps running while the video plays.
  3. Stop the recording, come back to Sage, then tap Import the recording below.
